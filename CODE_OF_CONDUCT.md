# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Veydria to be a professional, welcoming, and harassment free project for
everyone who works on it, regardless of experience, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful. Assume good intent and give constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect differing viewpoints and resolve disagreements with facts and
  reasoning.
- Keep discussion on topic and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional workplace.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull
requests, and any private or public channel where someone represents the
project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, report it to
conduct@veydria.com. Reports are handled discreetly. We will review every
report, respond as fast as we reasonably can, and respect the privacy of the
reporter.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding this code. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not
follow it, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they judge
to be harmful.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from common open source practice, including the
Contributor Covenant.
